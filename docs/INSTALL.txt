STORAGEWARDEN - START HERE
Free. Open source. Privacy first.

WHAT YOU NEED
A Mac with macOS 15 or newer, using Apple silicon or Intel.
No account, subscription or Xcode installation is needed.

INSTALL
1. Download the DMG from the official StorageWarden release page:
   https://github.com/Sjayeshkumar/StorageWarden/releases/tag/v0.2.2
2. Double-click the DMG.
3. Drag StorageWarden onto the Applications folder in the disk window.
4. Open Applications and double-click StorageWarden.
5. Eject the StorageWarden disk image in Finder when you are finished.

IMPORTANT: THIS IS AN EARLY PREVIEW
This build is ad-hoc signed. It is not Developer ID signed or notarized
by Apple. macOS may block it because it cannot verify the developer.
A successful download does not mean Apple has checked this app.

Only proceed if you trust this official source and accept the risk.
After an attempted launch, macOS may offer an app-specific Open Anyway
button in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Read the warning before
using it. If you are unsure, cancel and wait for a notarized release.
Do not disable Gatekeeper, remove quarantine using Terminal, or bypass
warnings saying an app contains malware or will damage your computer.
A managed work or school Mac may not allow an exception.
Apple's explanation: https://support.apple.com/102445

YOUR FIRST SCAN
Read the introduction, then choose a familiar folder to scan.
Explore the results, click folders to look inside, and preview files.
Review cleanup warnings before moving individual files to Trash.
Whole folders and app bundles cannot be moved by this security release.
Rescan older results before cleanup. Changed files, links and cross-drive
moves are refused. Removed files go into private StorageWarden folders
inside Trash; restore them by dragging them out in Finder, not Put Back.
Nothing is removed automatically. Emptying Trash is a separate choice.

Full Disk Access is optional. If needed, enable StorageWarden in
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access, then quit and
reopen the app. Some locations may still be unavailable.

EVERYDAY USE
Your last scan is saved on your Mac. While the app runs, changed folders
are updated in the background. Changes made while the app is quit need
a manual rescan. Closing the window keeps the menu-bar app running;
choose Quit to stop it. Starting at login is optional in Settings.
Click the shield in the menu bar to see activity readings.

YOUR PRIVACY
StorageWarden does not send files, scan results or activity readings
to its developer or any server. No ads, accounts, tracking or analytics.
Saved scans stay on your Mac. Activity readings are not saved as history.
Website links open in your browser. Backups and cloud services follow
your own settings, independently of this app. See PRIVACY.txt for details.

REMOVE THE APP
Quit StorageWarden, then move it from Applications to Trash.
To remove its saved scan too, follow the instructions in PRIVACY.txt.
